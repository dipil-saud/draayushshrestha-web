# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two co-primary audiences, both real and weighted equally:

1. **Patients in and around Nepal (Kathmandu).** Job: find a credible orthopaedic and spine surgeon, understand whether their condition is treated, and reach the clinic to book. They arrive under some physical concern and need to establish trust quickly, then act.
2. **Referring clinicians and professional peers.** Job: assess credentials, scope of practice, affiliations, and specialization, then contact for referral or collaboration.

## Product Purpose

A professional credibility and patient-education web presence for Dr. Aayush Shrestha. Success: within seconds, either audience can establish trust, grasp the scope of practice, and reach the clinic; a patient can also educate themselves on conditions and recovery through authored guides.

## Positioning

Nepal-based orthopaedic and spine surgeon with 15+ years of practice. Spine surgery fellowship at Grande International Hospital (affiliated with Spine Health International, Florida) and training in endoscopic spine surgery. Practises across Grande International Hospital and Pacific Multispeciality Clinic, Kupondole. Broad musculoskeletal scope: spine, shoulder, knee, foot and ankle, elbow, hip, hand and wrist, rheumatic conditions, and trauma.

## Operating Context

- Booking happens by phone or WhatsApp — there is no contact form, and conversion routes through `tel:`, `wa.me`, and `mailto` links.
- Clinics: Grande International Hospital; Pacific Multispeciality Clinic, Kupondole.
- ~85 conditions are documented across nine body regions; 12 authored patient-education guides cover wound care, exercise, spine/knee/neck health, disc herniation, and myths.
- Nepal audience implies variable connectivity and a wide device range; the site stays static, low-JS, and fast. English copy.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Astro site; no JS framework or hydration. One global stylesheet, one 45-line client script, two page-scoped scripts.
- Conversion is call/WhatsApp/email only — no form to harden.
- Must remain responsive, accessible (WCAG AA), and low-data friendly.
- Real named patient testimonials, real affiliation logos, real credentials, and real photography exist and must be used (not fabricated).

## Brand Commitments

Nothing is locked in the original identity, but a standing preference was set during the redesign: the visual world is **conventional / canon** (the category-standard medical-credibility site, played straight), with **Mayo Clinic** named as the peer reference setting the craft bar and palette register — clean cool white, deep clinical navy, serif-meets-sans editorial. Expressive or arts-and-crafts worlds (Wayfinding/saffron, Lokta Press, Raku) were offered and declined. Fixed facts only: the name "Dr. Aayush Shrestha", the practice details, credentials, affiliations, and testimonials.

## Evidence on Hand

- Four real patient testimonials, named, with portrait photos (patient-ojesh, patient-sabin, patient-arjesh, patient-prajali).
- Three real affiliation logos: Grande International Hospital, Pacific Clinic, Shankhamul Healthcare.
- Real credentials and training history (Bishop Cotton School; University of Dhaka MBBS; Kathmandu University MS Orthopaedics; spine fellowship, Spine Health International Florida).
- Real photography: doctor portraits (studio, white-coat), clinical/conference imagery.
- 12 authored patient guides; ~85 condition entries with descriptions.
- Image credits documented for third-party imagery (Wikimedia/Flickr, CC BY / CC BY-SA).

Do NOT fabricate testimonials, credentials, outcomes, or patient names.

## Product Principles

1. **Trust before persuasion.** Every claim rests on real evidence — credentials, affiliations, named patients — never assertion.
2. **Clarity for two audiences.** Patient comprehension and clinician credibility coexist; neither dumbs down nor loads jargon.
3. **Education as proof.** The condition library and guides demonstrate expertise rather than declaring it.
4. **Reachable by default.** The path to contact and booking is always one tap; in Nepal, phone and WhatsApp matter most.
5. **Composure suits medicine.** Confidence is earned through restraint and precision, not spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA is the floor (the incumbent site already meets it). Variable connectivity and a wide device range in Nepal require the redesign to stay fast and low-data; touch targets and contrast remain non-negotiable.
